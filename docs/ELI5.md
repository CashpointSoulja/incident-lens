# Explain it simply

## ELI5

When a salesperson at incident.io is about to talk to a new company, they want to know how that company deals with things breaking. Incident Lens reads the company's public web pages for them - the homepage, status page, blog and jobs pages - and writes down what it found, with a link to where it found it. Then it makes a few careful guesses, clearly marked as guesses, about what might help that company. It shows which incident.io products fit, plays out a pretend outage, and lets the salesperson and the customer change the numbers on what it could be worth. It never makes things up: if a page does not say it, Incident Lens does not claim it.

## 30 seconds, out loud

"Incident Lens is an internal tool for incident.io's revenue team. You type in a prospect's domain and in a couple of seconds you get a reliability brief: what their public pages actually say about how they run incidents - every claim linked and dated - a few hypotheses clearly marked as hypotheses, the incident.io products that fit and why, a walkthrough of an incident in their stack, and a business case you can edit live on the call. One tap turns it into a prospect-safe page with a link that keeps the numbers. It is live in production, it is tested, it fails closed rather than guessing, and it has a kill switch. That is the job as I understand it: build the software the revenue team relies on, and own it when it breaks."
