# Personas

These are working archetypes built from public descriptions of revenue roles and from how each role would use Incident Lens. They are **not** based on interviews with incident.io staff, and no quotes are attributed to real people. A pilot would replace them with validated personas.

## Account Executive (AE): primary user

- **Goal:** win technical evaluations with a point of view the buyer respects.
- **Today:** 20 to 40 minutes of tab-hopping before an important call, or going in generic.
- **Pain:** a claim they cannot back up costs credibility with an engineering buyer.
- **Uses:** evidence ledger, brief, scenario on screen-share, live ROI edits, share link after the call.
- **Trust breaker:** a single unsourced or wrong "fact" about the prospect.
- **Jobs:** J1 to J4 in [JTBD](JTBD.md).

## Business Development Rep (BDR)

- **Goal:** qualify quickly and book meetings with a relevant opener.
- **Today:** skims the homepage and LinkedIn, then sends a templated message.
- **Pain:** does not know whether a company has a reliability function worth talking to.
- **Uses:** signals and hypotheses as a qualification read, and one specific detail for the opener (written by the BDR; Incident Lens sends nothing).
- **Trust breaker:** slow lookups, or signals that turn out to be wrong.
- **Jobs:** J5, J4 (hand-off to the AE).

## Customer Success Manager (CSM)

- **Goal:** grow existing accounts without a hard sell.
- **Today:** relies on the account history in the CRM and what the customer says.
- **Pain:** misses public signals that an account has adopted new infrastructure or tools.
- **Uses:** the product and integration map with stated reasons, and the scenario to show an unused capability.
- **Trust breaker:** the tool claiming customer status it cannot know. Live lookups always show "Customer status not publicly confirmed".
- **Jobs:** J6.

## Product marketer

- **Goal:** sales-facing claims that are consistent, accurate and on-brand.
- **Today:** reviews decks and one-pagers after the fact.
- **Pain:** sellers paraphrasing product capabilities loosely.
- **Uses:** the product knowledge base sourced from incident.io's own pages, and share mode as the external surface.
- **Trust breaker:** off-brand or overclaiming copy in the share view.
- **Jobs:** J7.

## RevOps owner: buyer and operator

- **Goal:** tools the team actually uses, with clean data and no surprises.
- **Today:** evaluates tools on adoption, data hygiene and integration cost.
- **Pain:** shadow tools that write junk into the CRM or break silently.
- **Uses:** brief versions, the matcher and ROI rules versions, the CRM-shaped payload preview, the kill switch and the [runbook](RUNBOOK.md).
- **Trust breaker:** no owner, no monitoring, or numbers presented as guarantees.
- **Jobs:** J8.
